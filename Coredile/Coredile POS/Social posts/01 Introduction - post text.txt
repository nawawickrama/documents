Today we're introducing Coredile POS.

In most Sri Lankan businesses, the till prints the bill and then the real work starts. Someone counts stock on a Sunday. Someone types the day's sales into Excel. At the end of the month the accountant asks for a carrier bag full of paper.

Coredile POS does what comes after the bill. Every sale takes its own stock off and writes itself into a proper set of accounts. Supplier orders, deliveries and what you owe sit in the same place. Each month there's a VAT and SSCL summary ready for your accountant to check and file. And the tills keep working when the internet doesn't.
