# Coredile

Our main brand. Coredile builds software products; the folders here are its products and its own marketing.

| Folder | What it is | Start with |
|---|---|---|
| `Coredile website/` | coredile.com, the company site and blog (Astro, deployed to Hostinger) | its `README.md` |
| `Coredile POS/` | Multi-industry, offline-first POS (coredilepos.cloud) | `Coredile POS/README.md` |
| `Let's Ride/` | Trip-bidding app for tourists and drivers in Sri Lanka (letsride.lk) | `Let's Ride/README.md` |
| `Content automation/` | Writes, publishes and shares a coredile.com article four mornings a week | its `README.md` |
| `Social Media Marketing/` | Finished creatives for the Coredile brand (e.g. Villa Campaign) | |

keeps its own colours (`src/brand.ts` in its Video studio).
