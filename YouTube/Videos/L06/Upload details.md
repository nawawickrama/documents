## L06 – the long video

**Schedule:** Saturday 14 November 2026, **15:00 Central European Time** (19:30 in Sri Lanka). Time zone: (GMT+01:00) Berlin.

**Title**

```
Electricity Prices in Europe, 2008–2026: Who Pays the Most?
```

Title B (Test & compare, with thumbnail B):

```
Ireland Now Pays the EU's Highest Electricity Prices
```

**Description**

```
What households pay for a kilowatt-hour of electricity in every EU country, every six months from 2008 to early 2026, with all taxes included. The 2022 gas price shock, who pays the most now, how much of the rise is just inflation, what a price is made of, and what it means for a year's bill.

Sources
- Eurostat, Electricity prices for household consumers, bi-annual data (nrg_pc_204), updated 19 October 2026. https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/nrg_pc_204/default/table
- Eurostat, Electricity prices by type of user and price component (nrg_pc_204_c), 2025.
- Eurostat, Harmonised index of consumer prices (prc_hicp_midx), EU, 2008 and 2025: for the inflation comparison.
What we did to the data: prices are for households using 2,500 to 4,999 kWh a year, in euros per kWh, with all taxes and levies, and from 2021 with government discounts and credits. They are not adjusted for inflation, except where we say so. The "after inflation" figure compares the yearly averages for 2008 and 2025 with EU consumer prices. The yearly bill is an illustration: 3,500 kWh at each country's average price, without standing charges. Prices outside the euro area also move with exchange rates.
```

## S14 – Short (Sunday 15 November, 19:00 CET)

**Title:** `Denmark's electricity hit 59 cents in 2022`

**Description**

```
In the second half of 2022, Danish households paid 59 cents for a kilowatt-hour of electricity: more than twice the 2008 price. The EU average peaked later, at 29 cents in early 2024. Now Denmark has cut its electricity tax almost to zero for 2026 and 2027, and pays 24 cents, less than the EU average of 28.

Source: Eurostat (nrg_pc_204), all taxes included.
```
