**Schedule:** Wednesday 4 November 2026, **17:00 Central European Time** (21:30 in Sri Lanka). Time zone: (GMT+01:00) Berlin.

**Title**

```
Minimum Wage in Every EU Country (2026): Euros vs Real Buying Power
```

Title B (Test & compare, with thumbnail B):

**Description**

```
Gross monthly minimum wages in the 22 EU countries that have one, as of July 2026. First in euros, then in purchasing power standards, which take out price differences. The gap halves, Luxembourg loses first place, Romania passes Portugal and Greece, and the east has roughly tripled its minimum wages since 2016.

Sources
- Eurostat, Monthly minimum wages, bi-annual data (earn_mw_cur), July 2026 and every July since 2016, last updated 31 July 2026. https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/earn_mw_cur/default/table
What we did to the data: gross monthly minimum wages in July of each year. PPS = purchasing power standards, Eurostat's unit that takes out price differences between countries. Where workers get 14 payments a year, Eurostat spreads them over 12 months. Austria, Denmark, Finland, Italy and Sweden have no national minimum wage; Cyprus has had one since 2023. Euro figures are not adjusted for inflation.
```
