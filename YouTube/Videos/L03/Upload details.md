```
How Poland Caught Up: 35 Years in Numbers (1990–2025)
```

**Description**

```
Poland's GDP per person, adjusted for inflation and prices, from 1990 to 2025: from less than a third of Germany's level to three quarters. The moments that mattered, the countries Poland overtook, and what the line doesn't show: long hours, a growing gap between regions and a shrinking population.
```
