**Description**

```
How long a newborn can expect to live in 40 European countries, from 1950 to 2023. How Spain and Italy went from the middle of the pack to the top, the collapse in the east in the 1990s, the pandemic dip, and why Germany spends far more on health than Spain but lives two years less.
```
