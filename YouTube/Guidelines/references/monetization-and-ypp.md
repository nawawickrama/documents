## 1. What it takes to join today (until 31 January 2027)

To share in ad revenue, a channel needs **1,000 subscribers** and **one** of:

- **4,000 qualified public watch hours** in the last 12 months. Only public long-form videos count. Shorts feed watch time does not count.
- **10 million qualified Shorts views** in the last 90 days. Only public Shorts watched in the Shorts feed count.

And all of these:

- follows the YouTube channel monetisation policies (see `originality-for-data-videos.md`);
- lives in a country where YPP is available (Sri Lanka is);
- no active Community Guidelines strikes;
- 2-Step Verification on the Google account;
- access to advanced features on YouTube (phone verification or channel history);
- one active AdSense for YouTube account linked to the channel (can be set up from YouTube Studio).

Meeting the numbers only starts a review. It does not guarantee entry.

Unlisted, private and deleted videos do not count. Views from bots or paid promotion do not count and can get the channel refused.

## 2. What changes on 1 February 2027

Announced by YouTube on 10 August 2026 (blog post in `sources.md`).

- **New applicants** need 8,000 qualified watch hours in 365 days **or** 20 million qualified Shorts views in 90 days. Reporting says the 1,000-subscriber rule stays; YouTube's own post does not restate it.
- **Channels already in YPP are not affected** by the new entry bar. Existing partners must accept the updated terms in YouTube Studio; YouTube's help page says by 31 January 2027.
- **Shorts revenue for everyone** (ads and Premium) needs 10 million qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days, measured monthly. Fall below it and Shorts revenue pauses; long-form keeps earning. It restarts automatically when the channel is back above the line.
- **New Shorts ad format:** ads targeted at five or fewer channels pay the creator 45 percent directly, on top of the Shorts pool.
- **Fan funding and Shopping thresholds are unchanged** (but the fan funding tier is not available in Sri Lanka anyway).
- **Reported, not confirmed by YouTube's own page:** an activity test for existing partners (for example 1,000 watch hours a year, 1 million Shorts views in 90 days, or two long-form uploads or five Shorts every 90 days). Treat as likely and check the terms when they appear in Studio.
- **Not stated anywhere:** how applications already under review on 1 February 2027 are judged. One analysis says applications are judged against the rules in force when reviewed. Do not count on the old bar unless the channel qualifies and is reviewed before that date. Review "typically" takes about a month.

## 3. Sri Lanka specifics

- YPP (full tier) is available in Sri Lanka.
- The expanded YPP (500 subscribers, 3 uploads, 3,000 hours or 3 million Shorts views, for memberships, Super Thanks and Shopping) is **not** available in Sri Lanka. There is no early step on the ladder.
- Ad rates depend on where viewers are, not where the creator is. Viewers in the US, UK, Canada, Australia and Western Europe pay many times more than viewers in South Asia. English-language videos about global topics reach those audiences; Sinhala or Tamil content reaches a lower-paying audience.

## 4. The review, refusal and appeal

What reviewers look at (from the monetisation policy page): the channel's main theme, most-viewed videos, newest videos, the biggest share of watch time, video metadata (titles, thumbnails, descriptions) and the About section. The policies apply to the whole channel, not one video.

Since 10 March 2025, some reviews include extra checks, sometimes by a person.

If refused:

- The email names the policy, usually "reused content" or "inauthentic content" for a channel like ours.
- **Appeal** within 21 days from the Earn section of YouTube Studio. The appeal is normally an unlisted video (under five minutes) showing how we make the content: the data work, the script, the edit. A decision arrives in about 14 days. For a data channel this is a strength if we really do the analysis; record it as we go.
- **Or reapply** after 30 days, having fixed the channel. Later refusals can mean a longer wait (90 days is reported).
- Ignore anyone selling "appeal services" or a removal of the flag for money.

After joining, monetisation can be suspended for the same reasons. Making a new channel to get around a suspension can get all our channels terminated.

## 5. AdSense for YouTube, payment and tax

- **One AdSense account per payee name.** Use the owner's real legal name and address exactly as on the bank account. A second account in the same name gets refused.
- **An AdSense account approved earlier for a website can be linked** at YPP sign-up instead of opening a new one; its YouTube earnings go to a separate payments account inside it. It does **not** skip or lower the YPP thresholds or the channel review. The same AdSense account can be linked to more than one of our channels, so it does not decide which channel we use.
- **Check an old AdSense account before relying on it:** sign in and look for its status and any policy notices. An account with no activity for a long time may have been deactivated and can usually be reactivated from the sign-in page. An account closed for invalid traffic or another policy reason cannot be reopened or replaced, and blocks monetisation under that name.
- **Address check by post:** when earnings reach the verification threshold, Google posts a six-digit PIN to the payment address. It can take weeks to reach Sri Lanka. Enter it before the deadline (four months from when it was generated); three wrong entries stop payments. A replacement PIN can be requested.
- **Payout threshold:** US$100 (or the local equivalent). Payment goes to a bank account by wire transfer once the balance passes it.
- **US tax (on the money earned from US viewers):** submit tax information in AdSense (form W-8BEN for an individual). Without it, Google can withhold up to 24 percent of **all** worldwide YouTube earnings. With it, only US-viewer earnings are taxed, and the US–Sri Lanka tax treaty may lower the rate on those (the royalty rate is reported as 10 percent; confirm in IRS Publication 901). Claiming the treaty needs the owner's Sri Lankan tax identification number.
- **Sri Lankan tax:** YouTube income is the owner's income in Sri Lanka. Ask the accountant how it is taxed and how the US withholding is credited.

## 6. How much the formats actually pay

These are third-party 2026 estimates, not YouTube figures. Use them for planning only.

- **Shorts** pay roughly US$0.03 to $0.20 per 1,000 views. 10 million Shorts views might earn a few hundred to about two thousand dollars.
- **Long-form** education videos have a median RPM around US$10 per 1,000 views, with a wide spread ($2 to $20). Finance-adjacent topics (economies, wealth, companies) tend to pay more.
- How the Shorts pool works: Shorts feed ad revenue is pooled monthly. If a Short uses music from YouTube's music partners, part of that Short's share goes to music licensing (one track: half to the creator pool; two tracks: a third). The pool is split by each creator's share of engaged views, and the creator keeps 45 percent. Views from non-original Shorts, fake views and unsuitable content are excluded.
- **Using no licensed partner music in a Short keeps the whole share in the creator pool.** Audio Library tracks and our own audio are not partner music.

## 7. What this means for our plan

- Shorts are a subscriber engine, not an income source. Every Short should give a reason to watch a long-form video.
- The money and the 4,000 hours come from long-form (8 to 20 minutes holds watch time well for data stories).
- The channel has until about the end of December 2026 to cross the current bar and be reviewed before the 1 February 2027 change. If it does not, the target becomes 8,000 hours or 20 million Shorts views.
- 100,000 subscribers in 90 days is far above what most new channels reach. Track the real gates: 1,000 subscribers, 4,000 hours (or 10 million Shorts views), and a channel that would pass review.
