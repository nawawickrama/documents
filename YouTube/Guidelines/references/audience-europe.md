# Audience: Europe first

We make videos for viewers in **Europe first** (UK, Ireland, Germany, Austria, Switzerland, the Netherlands, Belgium, the Nordic countries, France and the rest of the EU), then the **US, Canada and Australia**. We do **not** aim at Sri Lanka or South Asia. Anyone is welcome to watch; we just don't make or promote videos for those markets.

## 1. Why

Ad income depends on where the **viewer** is, not where the creator lives. Advertisers pay many times more for viewers in Western Europe, North America and Australia than for viewers in South Asia. The same 10,000 views can earn ten times more or less depending on the audience mix.

## 2. What must not change

- **"Country of residence" in YouTube Studio stays Sri Lanka.** It must be where the owner actually lives. It decides YPP availability, tax and payments, not who sees the videos. Setting a false country breaks YouTube's terms and can cost the channel its monetisation.
- AdSense name and address stay the owner's real ones in Sri Lanka.

## 3. Language and style

- **English, written for people whose first language may not be English.** Short sentences, no slang, no idioms that do not translate. Most of our European viewers speak English as a second language.
- **British spelling** (colour, metre, programme), used consistently across titles, descriptions and on-screen text.
- **Metric units.** Money in euros, with US dollars where the source uses them; say which year's money. Dates as 2 November 2026. 24-hour time if a time appears.
- Write large numbers as "€1.2 billion" or "1.2 bn", not "1,2" or "1.200" which confuse readers from different countries.
- Flags and country names on screen help viewers who read slowly in English.
- **Captions:** upload or correct English captions on every video.
- **Translations:** in YouTube Studio, under Languages, add translated titles and descriptions in German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Polish and Portuguese. They appear to viewers in those languages and in their searches. Use auto-dubbing or multi-language audio when the channel has it, and check the result before publishing.
- Narration voice: Kokoro `af_heart`, an American woman's voice, chosen by the owner on 2 November 2026 for its quality. The accent is American, but on-screen text, titles and descriptions stay in British spelling. Write narration so numbers read naturally aloud ("1.2 trillion dollars", not "$1.2T").

## 4. Topics

Good for this audience: European countries compared (wages, rent, house prices, energy, taxes, life expectancy), the EU, football and other European sports, cars and manufacturing, European history over time, big world rankings told with a European angle, the US and China seen from Europe.

Avoid: topics only Sri Lankan or South Asian viewers would care about, and Sinhala or Tamil titles, tags or keywords. Clear the old Sinhala programming keywords from the channel settings.

Handle carefully (neutral, sourced, see `data-and-visuals.md`): maps with disputed borders (Kosovo, Crimea, Northern Cyprus), the World Wars, migration, elections.

## 5. Publishing times

Publish when Europe comes home from work: **16:00–18:00 CET** on weekdays (15:00–17:00 in the UK), mornings on weekends. The US East Coast is six hours behind CET, so this also catches its lunchtime.

From Sri Lanka that is **20:30–22:30 Sri Lanka time** in winter (CET is 4½ hours behind) and **19:30–21:30** in European summer time (CEST is 3½ hours behind). Use scheduled publishing rather than uploading live.

## 6. Promotion and early viewers

YouTube learns who to recommend a video to from the people who watch it first. If the first viewers are friends and local groups in Sri Lanka, YouTube shows the video to more people like them, and both reach and income suffer.

- Do not share new videos in Sri Lankan WhatsApp, Facebook or Telegram groups, or ask friends and family to watch "to help".
- Share, if at all, where the target audience is: relevant European subreddits that allow it, data-visualisation communities, and topic forums. Follow each community's self-promotion rules.
- If we ever pay for Google Ads promotion, target only our countries. Paid views help little toward the YPP watch hours anyway.

## 7. Checking it is working

In YouTube Analytics, Audience tab, look at Top geographies and Top languages for watch time, every week. The goal is most watch time from our countries. If South Asia grows large, check what drove it (a topic, a share, a tag) and stop doing that.
