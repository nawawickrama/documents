# Terrachart – channel setup

Everything about how the channel looks and is set up, in one place. The images in this folder are made from `source/assets.html`; run `node source/render.mjs` to rebuild them after a change.

## Name

- **Channel name:** Terrachart
- **Handle:** @terrachart
- **Tagline:** The world, charted through time.
- **What we make:** rankings, timelines and data stories about Europe and the world, for viewers in Europe first, then North America and Australia.
- **Why the name:** *terra* is Latin for the Earth and reads the same in most European languages. *Chart* is what we make. When we checked on 2 November 2026, no other channel or brand used it.

## Logo

A globe with a ranking inside it. The amber bar at the top is the leader, and it breaks out of the world.

## About text (channel description)

> Terrachart turns the world's numbers into short stories you can watch.
>
> How did Europe's biggest economies swap places since 1960? Which countries pay the best wages once you take off the rent? Where do people live longest, and what changed? Each video takes real data from sources like Eurostat, the World Bank, the OECD and the UN, and explains what moved and why. Not just bars racing across the screen.
>
> New rankings, timelines and data stories every week.
>
> Every source is listed under each video. Spotted a mistake, or want to see a chart we haven't made? Tell us in the comments.
